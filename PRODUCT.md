# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Inferred from the 8 November 2026 editorial brief and the existing static site. No question tool was available in this session, so these facts were not re-confirmed in a live interview.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and a small JavaScript module on GitHub Pages. No framework. Project pages are served under the `/mission-critical-site/` prefix, so links and assets stay relative.

## Users

Readers deciding whether Mission Critical is worth their time. They want a checkable account of the materials behind the AI build-out, then a path to the published issue and the Beehiiv subscribe page.

## Product Purpose

Mission Critical is Cam Drummond's newsletter site. It states what the newsletter follows, shows the one published issue, and sends readers to Beehiiv to read or subscribe.

## Positioning

The site shows the supply chain, the source, and the date, and it links to the canonical Beehiiv issue instead of reprinting it.

## Operating Context

Public pages are Home, Read, and About. The copper issue and subscribe flow live on Beehiiv. Issue 2, published 2 November 2026, is the only issue the site may list.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Strapline, kept intact: "What the AI build-out is made of". The compound "build-out" stays on one line, with a deliberate break before "is made of".
- Subscribe URL: https://itsmissioncritical.beehiiv.com/subscribe
- Copper issue URL: https://itsmissioncritical.beehiiv.com/p/copper-s-got-a-plumbing-problem
- Publication home: https://itsmissioncritical.beehiiv.com/
- The retired Beehiiv hostname is not a destination. Links use the itsmissioncritical publication only.
- Do not publish, deploy, edit Beehiiv, send mail, or submit a signup.
- Do not invent further issues, a cadence, reading time, audience size, biography, holdings, or a standing "no positions" line. Issue 2 has no verified holdings confirmation, so the site states none.
- Held drafts never appear in pages, metadata, feeds, or the sitemap.
- Hero motion uses the existing film and poster. It is designed motion, not documentary footage. No invented video credit.
- Video stays muted. Reduced motion starts on the still poster. Prolonged autoplay has a visible keyboard pause control. Reading text is not a motion field.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mission Critical
- Author, as published copy: Cam Drummond
- Strapline: What the AI build-out is made of
- Existing mark, film, poster, and section illustrations stay in the copper, cyan, and violet family
- Voice is sourced and dated. The public copy in the brief is the only wording added to the pages.

## Evidence on Hand

- One published web issue: "Copper's got a plumbing problem" (2 November 2026), with market figures dated 22–29 October 2026.
- One dated source example: Shanghai Metals Market, 28 October 2026, average Yangshan warrant premium of US$205 a tonne.
- Assets already in `art/`: logo, hero film (webm and mp4), hero poster, three illustrations, social image.
- Absent and not to be invented: further issues, reader counts, testimonials, biography, employer, and a confirmed issue-2 holdings line.

## Product Principles

- Show the source and the date.
- Link out to the canonical issue rather than duplicating it.
- One published issue until another is actually published.
- Motion may carry the hero film. It may not hide, move, or decorate the reading.
- Identity stays. The strapline is not rewritten to solve a layout problem.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable body type, visible focus, contrast at body and large-text levels, keyboard access to navigation and the motion control, a still first state when reduced motion is requested, and no sound on autoplay.
